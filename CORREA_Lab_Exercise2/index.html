About Us

Who We Are

Hostile Garage was established in 2017, starting out as a small motor parts shop built on trust and quality. Over the years, we have grown into a name that riders rely on for genuine, high-performance engine parts and upgrades. With years of experience in the motor parts industry, we have earned the trust of our customers by consistently delivering good quality products and honest, reliable service.

Our Mission

To provide riders with durable, high-quality engine parts at fair prices, backed by years of hands-on experience and a genuine commitment to customer satisfaction.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted motor parts shop in the region, known for our quality products, reliability, and years of proven experience serving the riding community.
